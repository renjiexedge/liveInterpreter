XEDGE LIVE INTERPRETER - USAGE AGREEMENT (1.0.0 Alpha)

1. What this is
   You are testing an early (alpha) version of Xedge Live Interpreter for
   Xedge Resource. It may not work correctly, may stop during a call, and may
   translate inaccurately. Do not rely on it as the only record of what was said.

2. Who may use it
   Only you, only on your work computer, only for Xedge Resource interviews.
   Do not share the installer or copy it to other computers.

3. The service key
   The app uses a Gemini service key provided by Xedge Resource.
   Enter it only in the app. Do not share, copy, photograph or post it. Usage is
   paid for by Xedge Resource, has a spending limit and is shared by all testers;
   if translation stops with a "service limit" message, tell Johnny.

4. Audio and privacy
   During translation, speech from you and the candidate is sent to Google's
   Gemini service to be translated. The app does not save audio or transcripts on
   your computer. When you tell the candidate the interview is recorded, also tell
   them a live translation tool is used that sends the audio to Google's
   translation service.
   Logs on your computer contain technical information only (device names,
   connection and audio-quality events), never what was said.

5. Bug reports
   When you send a bug report you will see exactly what is included before it is
   sent. Reports go to renjie.xedge@gmail.com and are used only to fix the app.

6. Other software you install
   The setup guide asks you to install VB-Cable and Voicemeeter Banana from
   VB-Audio's website. These are separate products under VB-Audio's own terms.
   Uninstalling Xedge Live Interpreter does not remove them.

7. Feedback
   Xedge Resource may use your feedback to improve the app, without obligation.

8. No warranty
   The app is provided "as is". Xedge Resource is not responsible for
   missed or incorrect translations or interrupted calls during the test.

9. Uninstalling
   To uninstall the app, go to Settings > Apps and
   choose to delete your settings and saved key.
